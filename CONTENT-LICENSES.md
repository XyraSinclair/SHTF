The [MIT license](LICENSE) covers this repository's structure, scripts,
playbooks, and documentation — the words we wrote. The books, manuals, and
guides included in or fetched by this repository are other people's works and
carry their own terms:

- **US government publications** (Army field manuals, FEMA guides, USDA
  guides, Peace Corps manuals, CDC/EPA/NREL/DOE documents, USGS maps):
  public domain as works of the US federal government (17 U.S.C. § 105).
- **WHO / UN publications**: distributed under WHO's open-access terms,
  generally CC BY-NC-SA — free to share and adapt for non-commercial use
  with attribution and share-alike. Check the notice inside each document.
- **Hesperian Health Guides** (*Where There Is No Doctor* family):
  Hesperian's open copyright permits non-commercial reproduction and
  distribution with attribution; commercial use requires permission. See
  hesperian.org for their current policy.
- **Project Gutenberg texts**: public domain in the United States; the
  Project Gutenberg trademark license applies only if you keep their header.
- **ARRL ARES field resources** (`ARRL_ARES_Field_Resources_Manual.pdf`,
  `ARRL_ARES_Plan_2025.pdf`): free public downloads from ARRL's Public
  Service pages. Other ARRL publications (including the *Emergency
  Communication Handbook*) are commercial products and are **not** included
  here.
- **Other freely distributed guides** (Sphere Handbook, Humanure materials,
  solarcookers.org, Agromisa/Agrodok, extension-service publications):
  distributed free by their publishers, most under non-commercial or
  attribution terms noted inside each file.

If you plan to redistribute this kit, the safe reading is: the repo scaffolding
is MIT; the library contents are redistributable for non-commercial
preparedness use; anything beyond that, check the individual work.

open an issue and it will be removed.
