## Comms protocol

1. **Call / text the hub first.** Long-distance often works when local doesn't.
2. State: "I'm [name], I'm [safe / hurt], I'm at [location], I plan to [next step]."
3. Proceed to the appropriate meeting point if you can't go home.
4. Do not tie up voice lines — one state-check-end call, then off.

### Code phrases (optional)

- **"Execute the plan"** = evacuate to primary destination.
- **"Need the red folder"** = grab the documents packet.
- **"I need a ride home"** = I feel unsafe, come get me.
