# Water Purification Card

**Any water of unknown origin needs treatment. Diarrheal illness from untreated water is a leading disaster killer.**

## Minimum daily water

- **Drinking**: 2 liters / person / day (more in heat or activity).
- **Hygiene + sanitation**: 2 liters / person / day additional.
- **Total**: 4 L / person / day baseline.

## Treatment = filtration + disinfection

**Filtration** removes particulates, protozoa (giardia, cryptosporidium), most bacteria.

**Disinfection** kills viruses and remaining bacteria that filtration misses.

Do **both** for water of unknown origin.

## Method 1: Boil

- Rolling boil for **1 minute** at sea level.
- **3 minutes** above 6,500 ft (2,000 m).
- Kills bacteria, viruses, protozoa.
- Does not remove chemical contamination, heavy metals, silt.
- Let settle, pour through cloth to remove sediment, then boil.

## Method 2: Chlorine bleach (household, unscented)

Per 1 liter (1 quart) of clear water:
- **2 drops** of regular 5–6% unscented household bleach.
- Stir, let stand **30 minutes**.
- Should have faint chlorine smell. If not, add 2 more drops, wait 15 more min.

Per 1 gallon:
- **8 drops** bleach.
- Same procedure.

For cloudy water: **double the bleach dose.**

Bleach loses potency over time — replace stored bleach every 6 months.

**Do not use scented, color-safe, or added-cleaner bleaches.**

## Method 3: Water purification tablets

Most common: Aquatabs (NaDCC) or Potable Aqua (iodine).

- Follow label for ratio (typically 1 tablet / liter).
- Wait time per label (usually 30 min, longer for cold water).
- Iodine not recommended for: pregnant women, thyroid conditions, long-term use (>2 weeks).
- Cheap, light, shelf-stable — keep some in go-bag.

## Method 4: Filter + disinfect

Popular filters:

- **Sawyer Squeeze / Mini**: 0.1 micron, removes protozoa + bacteria, does NOT remove viruses.
- **MSR Guardian**: removes viruses too. Expensive.
- **LifeStraw**: similar to Sawyer; doesn't remove viruses.

Filters alone:
- ✅ Clear running mountain water (typically no virus risk).
- ❌ Water from agricultural areas, downstream of settlements, flood water.

For suspect sources: **filter, then bleach or boil.**

## Method 5: Solar disinfection (SODIS)

- Clear PET bottle of clear water.
- Full sun, **6+ hours** (or 2 days if overcast).
- Kills most bacteria, viruses, protozoa.
- Backup method for when you have time and sun but no fuel.

## What doesn't work

- **Cloth filtering alone**: removes sediment, not pathogens.
- **UV pens (SteriPen)**: work, but only with clear water and functioning electronics.
- **"Letting water sit"**: settles sediment but doesn't disinfect.
- **Adding vinegar, salt, fruit**: marketing, not science.

## Water sources ranked (most to least safe)

1. Bottled, sealed, commercial.
2. Tap water before any utility disruption.
3. Water heater tank (30–50 gal potable reserve).
4. Toilet tank (not bowl) — **if no chemical cleaners used**.
5. Rain collected off clean roof (filter + disinfect).
6. Running wilderness stream above any human activity (filter + disinfect).
7. Still ponds, lakes (filter + disinfect; avoid algae blooms).
8. Agricultural or urban runoff (filter + disinfect, avoid if possible).
9. Flood water (do not drink, even treated — chemicals, sewage).
10. Pool / hot tub water (sanitation use only).

## Signs water is bad

- Visible sheen, oily surface.
- Strong chemical, sewage, or petroleum smell.
- Heavy green/brown algae scum.
- Dead animals near or in source.

Treatment cannot fix chemical contamination. Find different water.

## Storage

- Clean containers, **food-grade plastic** preferred.
- Keep cool, out of direct sunlight.
- Rotate tap-water stores every **6 months**.
- Commercial sealed water: 2+ years typical.

## Ice and snow

- **Melt before drinking.** Eating snow directly accelerates hypothermia and dehydrates you.
- Melt using body heat (bottle inside jacket), stove, or fire.
- Boil or disinfect melt water if source is unknown (especially near roadways or livestock).

## Emergency quick reference

| Situation | Fastest method |
|-----------|---------------|
| Clear water, have stove | Boil 1 min |
| Clear water, no stove | 2 drops bleach/L + 30 min |
| Cloudy water | Filter cloth → boil or 4 drops bleach/L + 30 min |
| Extended outage | Filter + disinfect |
| Fresh snow | Melt + disinfect if unsure source |
| Sealed bottled water | Drink |
