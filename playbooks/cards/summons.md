# Summons Card

**The card that finds you. Fill in, print 6-up, laminate, one in every wallet and on every phone lock screen.**

Six lines. If it doesn't fit on six lines, it's not a summons card.

---

## Template (fill in, then cut down)

```
ICE: ___________________________________  ___-___-____
MEET: _________________________________ (primary)
ME: ___________ Blood: ___  Allergy: _______________
BLEEDING → Limb spurting? TOURNIQUET high & tight. Else press hard.
WATER (clear) → 8 drops unscented bleach per gallon, wait 30 min.
WHAT NOW? → default STAY. Leave only if home is unsafe.
```

---

## Rules

- **Six lines, no more.** This is the card you look at when you cannot think.
- **Print 6-up on a letter sheet** (6 copies per page). Cut, laminate at FedEx/UPS (~$2 per card).
- **Screenshot it to your phone lock screen.** The right info has to find you at the moment of attention.
- One copy in each wallet, one in each go-bag, one on the fridge, one taped inside each car's glovebox.
- Re-issue annually. Old cards → shred.

The longer cards (`first-aid.md`, `stop-the-bleed.md`, `water-purification.md`, `radiation-shelter.md`) are the deep-dives. This card is the **summons** — it tells a scared person what to do in the first 10 seconds before they can read anything else.

## Why each line

1. **ICE contact** — Paramedics look for this. One number. The out-of-area hub from your comms plan.
2. **Meeting point** — Where your people go when phones don't work. Street address, not a vibe.
3. **You** — Name, blood type, #1 allergy. For someone else to read off you if you can't talk.
4. **Bleeding** — Leading preventable cause of death from trauma. Pressure first, tourniquet second.
5. **Water** — The one thing everyone runs out of first. Bleach dose is the only survival chemistry you must memorize.
6. **Stay or go** — The one decision that prevents the most regret. Default is stay; most emergencies end in hours.

Generate a personalized copy: `./tools-scripts/household-setup.sh` writes a filled-in version to your home directory.
