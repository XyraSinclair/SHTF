# Kids: What To Do

**If you only read one line: stop, think, find a safe adult, and do not hide.**

## 1. If you get lost

**Stop moving.**
- Stop when you know you are lost.
- Stay where you are.
- Hug a tree, sit by a tree, or sit in one clear spot.
- Do not keep walking to "fix it."

**Make yourself findable.**
- Put on bright clothes if you have them.
- Stay near open space if it is safe.
- Blow your whistle in bursts.
- Yell when you hear people.

**Stay warm and calm.**
- Put on your jacket before you get cold.
- Stay out of water.
- If you have a snack, eat a little.
- Tell yourself: "They are looking for me. My job is to stay put."

## 2. If there is a fire

**Get out.**
- Go when the smoke alarm sounds.
- Crawl low under smoke.
- Feel a door before opening it. If it is hot, use another way out.
- Close doors behind you.

**Never hide.**
- Do not hide in a closet.
- Do not hide under a bed.
- Firefighters may look and sound scary. They are helpers.
- Once outside, stay outside.

## 3. If someone is hurt

**Find an adult.**
- Say: "Someone is hurt. Come now."
- Point to the person.
- Tell what happened if you saw it.

**Do not touch blood.**
- Do not put your hands in blood.
- Do not move a person who fell hard unless fire, water, or a car is coming.
- Bring a clean towel, shirt, or first aid kit to an adult.
- If an adult tells you to call 911, say where you are first.

## 4. Your emergency info card

**Carry the card.**
- Keep one in your backpack.
- Keep one in your jacket.
- Keep one in your go-bag.

**Know what is on it.**
- Your full name.
- Your grown-up's name.
- One phone number.
- Allergies and medicine.
- Safe pickup people.

## 5. Who are the helpers

**Look for uniforms first.**
- Firefighter.
- Police officer.
- Paramedic.
- Park ranger.
- Security guard.

**If no uniform is there.**
- Go to a store counter, school office, library desk, clinic desk, or front desk.
- Look for a parent with kids.
- Say: "I am lost. I need help calling my grown-up."
- Do not leave with a stranger unless your grown-up told you that person is safe.
