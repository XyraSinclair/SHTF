# First-Aid Card

Priority order: **Airway → Breathing → Circulation → Disability → Exposure (ABCDE)**

## Universal response

1. Scene safe? Make it safe or move patient.
2. Gloves if available. Tap shoulders, shout: "Are you okay?"
3. No response → call 911 (or shout for someone else to). State location first.
4. Check breathing for **≤10 seconds**. Look, listen, feel.
5. Not breathing / gasping → **CPR** (see below).
6. Breathing → place in **recovery position** (on side, top leg bent forward, head tilted to open airway). Monitor.

## CPR (adult)

- Heel of hand in center of chest, other hand on top.
- **100–120 compressions/min**, **2 inches (5 cm) deep**, full recoil between.
- Rescue breaths optional if untrained; compression-only is effective.
- 30 compressions : 2 breaths if giving rescue breaths.
- Switch rescuers every 2 minutes if possible.
- Continue until: responsiveness returns, trained help arrives, exhaustion.

**Child**: one hand, ~1.5 inches deep. **Infant**: two fingers, ~1.5 inches deep.

## Choking (conscious adult)

1. "Are you choking?" If no sound/cough → 5 back blows between shoulder blades.
2. Still obstructed → 5 abdominal thrusts (Heimlich). Fist above navel, thumb in, grab with other hand, inward-and-upward thrust.
3. Alternate 5 and 5 until cleared or unresponsive.
4. If unresponsive → begin CPR, check mouth before breaths.

## Major bleeding

See [`stop-the-bleed.md`](stop-the-bleed.md). Short version:

1. Direct pressure, hard, keep going.
2. If won't stop and on a limb → tourniquet 2–3 in above wound, tighten until stops, note time.
3. Hemostatic gauze packed into wound if available.
4. Elevation if possible.

## Shock signs

Pale, cold clammy skin; rapid weak pulse; rapid shallow breathing; confusion; nausea.

- Lay flat.
- Legs elevated 6–12 in (unless head/chest injury).
- Warm (blanket).
- No food/water.
- Get to medical care.

## Burns

- Cool running water 10–20 min (not ice, not butter).
- Clean non-stick covering.
- Do not pop blisters.
- Medical care for: larger than palm, face/hands/feet/joints/genitals, any child.

## Hypothermia

Shivering, confusion, slurred speech, poor coordination.

- Move to warmth.
- Remove wet clothing.
- Dry layers, insulate.
- Warm sugary fluids if conscious.
- **Never**: alcohol, rapid hot immersion, rubbing extremities.

## Heat stroke

Hot skin (dry or wet), confusion, temp >103°F, possibly unconscious.

- **Call 911 immediately.**
- Cool aggressively: cold water immersion, wet sheets, cold packs at neck/armpits/groin.
- No fluids if unconscious.

## Seizure

- Clear area, don't restrain.
- Cushion head.
- Time it.
- After: recovery position.
- 911 if: first-ever, >5 min, back-to-back, injury, or pregnancy.

## Fracture (suspected)

- Do not move if neck/back possible.
- Immobilize joint above and below.
- Check circulation past injury (pulse, warmth, color).
- Ice, elevate.
- Get to care.

## Snakebite

- Keep bitten limb below heart.
- Remove jewelry before swelling.
- Mark swelling edge with pen and time.
- Get to care.
- **Do not**: cut, suck, tourniquet (unless known neurotoxic species, last resort), apply ice.

## Poisoning

Call **Poison Control: 1-800-222-1222**.

Do not induce vomiting unless instructed.
