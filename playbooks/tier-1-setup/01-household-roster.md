# Household Roster (template)

Fill this out once. Print it. Put a copy in your go-bag, your car, and with your out-of-area contact.

**Do not commit a filled-in copy to git.** This file contains sensitive personal information. Save your completed version as `household-roster-private.md` (gitignored) or offline on an encrypted USB.

## If you only read one line

**First responders, hospital staff, and out-of-area family cannot help effectively without basic facts about the people involved.** One page per person saves hours of guessing in a crisis.

## Review schedule

- **Annually**: re-read the whole document and update anything changed.
- **After any medical change**: new diagnosis, new medication, changed doctor → update same day.
- **After a move**: everything location-dependent changes. Redo within 48 hours.
- **When a child changes school / starts driving / leaves home**: update.

---

## Before it happens, do this once

1. Set a calendar reminder for the same weekend every year to review this document.
2. Save one copy printed, one copy on an encrypted USB, one copy in encrypted cloud (see [`04-cash-and-documents.md`](04-cash-and-documents.md)).
3. Tell your out-of-area contact they are the out-of-area contact, and what that means.
